1. Introduction

Borbworks Studio ("we", "us", or "our") built Chonky Borb Adventures as a free-to-play, offline-capable, ad-supported mobile game. This page informs players ("you") regarding our policies concerning the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you download, install, and play our game.

We respect your privacy. Our core philosophy is simple: we do not collect, store, or sell any personal data ourselves. The game functions fully offline, does not require an account, and stores your game progress strictly on your device. Only third-party advertising SDKs access the network to deliver ads when an internet connection is available.

By installing or playing Chonky Borb Adventures, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We (the Developer) Collect

We collect zero personal information directly from you.

  • No User Accounts: You do not need to register, create an account, log in with social media, or provide an email address, name, or phone number to play the game.
  • No Developer Servers: We do not operate external web servers, databases, or cloud accounts that store your identity, activity, or game data.
  • No In-App Purchases: The game contains no real-money transactions, payment processing, or financial data collection.

3. Local Save Data Storage

All game progress—including your high scores, collected Breadcrumbs and Golden Croissants, your Chonk rank, unlocked cosmetic skins, equipped skin, whether you have seen the story and tutorial, and audio/vibration preferences—is stored locally on your device in standard application storage (Application.persistentDataPath).

The first time you open the game, it asks what year you were born. Your birth year is stored with your save on your device and is used only to choose the right kind of ads (see Section 6). It is never sent to us or to our advertising partner.

  • Device-Only: This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.
  • Data Deletion: You have complete control over your local data. You can delete all save data at any time by clearing the app's cache and storage in your Android device settings (Settings > Apps > Chonky Borb Adventures > Storage > Clear Data) or by uninstalling the game.

4. Third-Party Advertising and Analytics

Chonky Borb Adventures is offered for free and is supported by in-game advertisements (specifically rewarded video ads for optional game bonuses and occasional interstitial ads shown between runs).

When your device is connected to the internet, our advertising partner, Google AdMob, may collect and process certain device and usage data to serve ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance. For players under 13, ads are child-directed and much more limited, as described in Section 6.

Data Collected by Advertising Networks

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect:

  • Device and Hardware Identifiers: Android Advertising ID (AAID / GAID), vendor identifiers, device model, operating system version, device carrier, language, and screen dimensions.
  • Coarse / Approximate Location: General geographic location (such as country or city level) inferred from your Internet Protocol (IP) address. The game never accesses or requests precise GPS location data.
  • App and Ad Interaction Data: Information about ads served, ad impressions, video playback completion, click-throughs, and user interactions with advertisements.
  • Diagnostic and Performance Data: SDK crash logs, launch time, and performance metrics used to troubleshoot and maintain ad stability.

Third-Party Privacy Policies

We encourage you to review Google's privacy policies:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and Swiss Users (GDPR Compliance)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland:

  • Consent Management Platform (CMP): Upon your first launch of the game with an active internet connection, you will be presented with a consent dialog powered by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Players under 13 are treated as under the age of consent: they are not shown this dialog and only receive non-personalized ads.
  • Your Choice: You can choose whether to consent to personalized advertisements and associated data processing by ad partners. If you decline consent, only non-personalized, contextual advertisements will be served.
  • Revoking or Changing Consent: You may review, modify, or revoke your advertising consent preferences at any time through the in-game Settings screen by selecting "Privacy options", or by resetting your device's advertising identifier in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Reset or Delete advertising ID).

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA and Global Standards)

Chonky Borb Adventures is a casual game for players of all ages, including children. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the Google Play Families Policy, and similar laws worldwide.

  • Age screen: The first time you open the game, it asks for your birth year with a neutral question. The answer stays on your device (see Section 3).
  • Players under 13: All ad requests are marked as child-directed and as coming from a user under the age of consent. Google AdMob then serves only non-personalized ads with a general-audience (G) rating, without interest-based advertising, remarketing, or the Android Advertising ID. AdMob is a Google Play Families Self-Certified ads SDK.
  • Before the age is known: The game requests no ads at all until the age question has been answered.
  • Everyone else: Ads are limited to a PG rating, because the game is made for all ages.
  • No personal information: We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, or from anyone, and the game has no accounts, chat, or sharing features.
  • Parents and guardians: If you believe a child's information has been handled incorrectly, please contact us at borbworks.studio@gmail.com and we will promptly help, including asking our advertising partner to delete any related data.

7. United States State Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

For residents of California and other US states with applicable consumer privacy laws:

  • We do not sell or share personal information for monetary consideration.
  • Third-party ad partners may process device identifiers and coarse location to deliver interest-based ads.
  • You can exercise your right to opt out of interest-based advertising by enabling "Opt out of Ads Personalization" or "Delete advertising ID" in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

8. Data Security and Transmission

  • Encryption in Transit: All network requests initiated by integrated advertising SDKs are transmitted using industry-standard HTTPS / Transport Layer Security (TLS) encryption.
  • No Remote Storage: Because we do not operate remote user databases or collect personal data, we do not store your personal information on external servers.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements, app functionality, or advertising partner implementations.

Any updates will be published at https://zoreladreanjava.github.io/chonky-borb-privacy/ with an updated "Effective Date" at the top of the policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
